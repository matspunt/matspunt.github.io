Some English poems written over the past six months or so. Various themes. Mostly about love. I can never fully explain why one day I want to write in Dutch and the next day I am more drawn to English. I suppose it's partly due to extreme globalisation (English being the lingua franca of the internet), and partly because I want to share my creative output with non-Dutch people in my inner circle. I'm learning Danish too, maybe in a few years I'll feel confident enough to try writing a poem in Danish. If you want to recognize LLM generated text, look for egregious use of em dashes (—), they seem infatuated with them (or emojis...). Something about LLM generated text makes me sad. Wish it wasn't so commonplace but this appears the new reality we live in. 

### Poems

*It's more important to be nice, I guess, than being wise*<br>

— Julian Casablancas<br>

**Advice to myself**<br>
Read books which are never read.<br>
Say things which are never thought.<br>
Write words which are never felt.<br>
Muse on nothing worth your time.<br>

Do not protect the whims of the heart -<br>
let them fly like a bird with bruised wings;<br>
they’ll land safely, carried by the wind,<br>
if they dare to leap from their hideout.<br>

Fallen leaves nourish the forest floor<br>
and, similarly, failed dreams should<br>
be a fire in the woodlands, cleansing<br>
a path for bigger and bolder aspirations.<br> 

Rarely tell the truth, spin a web of lies<br>
in which you get caught too: this is art.<br> 
Prefer abstraction over heavy realism.<br>
Don’t let ‘wise’ get in the way of ‘nice’.<br>

**blurred vision**<br>
evening, desire, merely<br>
wanting more taste,<br> 
more touch, your face<br>

half-forgotten dawn<br>
tastes of dew in desert<br>
sun soon evaporates<br>

heart too tender<br>
marinated yearning<br>
solitude - mallet<br>

straightens out fibers<br>
single-textured, single-<br>
minded, filled with<br>

love for you, only<br>
more love in absence<br>
only more love<br>

*to let the blood snail and thicken,*<br>
*as a second skin grows*<br>
*over these spineless dreams.*<br>

— Andrew Waterhouse<br>

**spineless dreams**<br>
sailing on waves of time<br>
superficial poetic imagery<br>

nimbly avoiding cliff faces<br>
aided by a dash of rum<br>

ship’s on the right course<br>
when it crashes on land<br>

poets their dreams strand<br>
as they fail to describe the<br>

sound of bow striking sand<br>

**Distance**<br>
My skin has not felt delight since your skin<br>
left mine. My eyes desperately crave the sight<br>
of your eyes - the ardour coming from within<br>
an elastic band that binds our bodies tight.<br>

With each passing day, separation expands.<br> 
Fires with decreased oxygen burn a lot longer<br>
and with each lost touch from your hands<br>
my adoration and desire only grow stronger.<br>

We might not spend each minute together<br>
but stretched bands tend to bounce back,<br>
and when parted, we’re connected by a tether<br>
that keeps our love intact, even as it cracks.<br>
